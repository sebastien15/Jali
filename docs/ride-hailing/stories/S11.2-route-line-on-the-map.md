## User story
**As a** rider or driver,
**I want** to see the route drawn on the map during pickup and trip,
**so that** I know where we are going.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Polyline from driver → pickup while arriving, pickup → destination during trip.
- [ ] Route refreshes when the driver deviates by more than 200 m.
- [ ] Passed and remaining parts are styled differently.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
