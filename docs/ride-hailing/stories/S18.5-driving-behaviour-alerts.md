## User story
**As a** admin,
**I want** to detect speeding and harsh braking during trips,
**so that** unsafe drivers are coached or removed.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Speed vs road limit (when available) and accelerometer events recorded per trip.
- [ ] Weekly safety score for drivers; repeated issues flagged.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
