## User story
**As a** rider,
**I want** to book a driver with their car for several hours and multiple stops,
**so that** errands, business days and tours are simple.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Packages of 2/4/8 hours with km allowance, priced from driver's hourly rates.
- [ ] Extra time and km beyond allowance charged automatically.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
