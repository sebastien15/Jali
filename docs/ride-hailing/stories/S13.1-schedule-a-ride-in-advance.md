## User story
**As a** rider,
**I want** to book a ride for a future date and time,
**so that** early flights and important meetings are covered.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Schedule from 30 min to 30 days ahead; quote shown and locked at booking.
- [ ] Driver can accept in advance, or dispatch starts 15 min before pickup if unassigned.
- [ ] Reminders to rider and driver; free cancellation until 60 min before (configurable).
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
