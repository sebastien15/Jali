## User story
**As a** hotel receptionist,
**I want** to book rides for guests who don't have the app,
**so that** guests get reliable transport.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Partner dashboard to book for a guest by name and phone; guest gets SMS with driver details.
- [ ] Billing to partner account or guest pays driver.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
