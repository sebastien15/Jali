## User story
**As a** parent,
**I want** to pay for my family members' rides and see their trips,
**so that** my family travels safely.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Invite members; their rides are charged to my payment method; I get trip notifications.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
