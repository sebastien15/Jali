## User story
**As a** customer,
**I want** the full price for my dates and the owner's terms before I request,
**so that** there are no surprises at pickup.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Price = days × daily price (+ owner extras), deposit shown separately; *No Jali fees* line.
- [ ] Terms: mileage limit, fuel policy, driver age/licence, cancellation rules, damage responsibility.
- [ ] The quote is snapshotted on the request.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
