## User story
**As a** user,
**I want** to delete my account and download my data from inside the app,
**so that** my privacy rights are respected (required by Apple and Google).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] *Delete account* in Profile with confirmation; data anonymised/deleted per retention rules; financial records kept as legally required.
- [ ] *Download my data* produces a JSON/PDF export by email.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
