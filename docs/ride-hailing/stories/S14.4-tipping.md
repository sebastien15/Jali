## User story
**As a** rider,
**I want** to tip my driver after the trip,
**so that** I can reward great service.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Preset amounts and custom; available up to 24 h after the trip.
- [ ] 100% of tips go to the driver (no commission); ledger records them.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added

## Notes
- Deferred: needs an owner decision (subsidy, wallet or loyalty funding) under the zero-fee policy.
