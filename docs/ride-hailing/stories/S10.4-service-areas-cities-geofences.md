## User story
**As a** superadmin,
**I want** to define service areas per city with their own guardrails and on/off switch,
**so that** we can launch Kigali first and add Musanze, Rubavu, Huye later.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Admin draws or uploads polygons for service areas and special zones (airport, stadium).
- [ ] Requests with pickup outside an active area get a clear *Not available here yet* message.
- [ ] Guardrails, commission and features can be overridden per city.
- [ ] Zones are reused by airport queue, pickup points and heatmap.
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
