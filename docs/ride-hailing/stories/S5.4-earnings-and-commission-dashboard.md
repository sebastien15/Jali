## User story
**As a** driver,
**I want** to see my earnings today/this week/this month and what I owe Jali,
**so that** I manage my income and settle on time.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `GET /driver/earnings` returns totals by period, trip count, commission owed and payout history.
- [ ] Drive tab reuses `WeekSummaryCard` with ride earnings included.
- [ ] When commission owed exceeds the threshold, I see a banner with how to settle (MoMo) and I can't go online.
- [ ] Strings translated (EN/FR/RW/SW) and colours from `C.xxx` where UI is touched
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, with ownership checks in the controller
