## User story
**As a** professional driver,
**I want** to set hourly/daily rates and list my skills (manual/automatic, languages, licence categories, years of experience),
**so that** customers who need a driver for their own car can find me.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Fields: hourly rate + minimum hours, daily rate + hours included, overtime per hour, out-of-town fee per day.
- [ ] Skills: transmissions, languages (Kinyarwanda, English, French, Swahili, other), licence categories, years of experience.
- [ ] Validation against superadmin guardrails for hire rates.
- [ ] Requires `offer-driver-hire` and verification; no vehicle required.
- [ ] Strings translated (EN/FR/RW/SW) and colours from `C.xxx` where UI is touched
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, with ownership checks in the controller
