## User story
**As a** private car owner or professional driver,
**I want** to sign up as a Jali driver and pick what I offer (rides, hire-a-driver, private seats, rentals),
**so that** I can earn with my car or my driving skills.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] From Profile, *Become a driver* opens onboarding with a clear checklist: profile → documents → vehicle → rates → submit.
- [ ] I can select one or more services; the checklist adapts (e.g. hire-a-driver does not require a vehicle).
- [ ] Progress is saved after each step; I can leave and resume.
- [ ] Until verified, I see status *Under review* and cannot go online.
- [ ] Available in EN / FR / RW / SW.
- [ ] Strings translated (EN/FR/RW/SW) and colours from `C.xxx` where UI is touched
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, with ownership checks in the controller
