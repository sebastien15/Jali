## User story
**As a** rider,
**I want** the driver's car to glide smoothly and turn in the right direction,
**so that** the app feels alive and precise like Uber.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Marker interpolates between location updates (no jumps) at 60 fps on a mid-range Android.
- [ ] Marker rotates with heading; snaps to road when routing data is available.
- [ ] Camera follows the car without fighting manual pan/zoom.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
