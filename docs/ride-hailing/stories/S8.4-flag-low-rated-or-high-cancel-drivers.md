## User story
**As a** admin,
**I want** drivers with low ratings or many cancellations flagged automatically,
**so that** quality stays high.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Drivers below 4.0 average after 20 rated trips, or above a configurable cancel rate, appear in a *Needs review* list.
- [ ] Admin can warn or suspend from the list; driver is notified.
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
