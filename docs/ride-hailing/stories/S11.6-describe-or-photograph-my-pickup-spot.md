## User story
**As a** rider,
**I want** to add a short note or a photo of where I'm standing,
**so that** the driver recognises the spot.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Optional text (140 chars) and one photo per ride, visible to the driver after accept.
- [ ] Photo deleted 24 h after ride completion.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
