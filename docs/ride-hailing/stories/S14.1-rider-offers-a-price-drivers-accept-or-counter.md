## User story
**As a** rider,
**I want** to propose my own price and receive driver counter-offers,
**so that** price is negotiated fairly, inDrive-style.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Rider sets an offer within guardrails; nearby drivers see it and can accept or counter once.
- [ ] Rider sees offers with driver rating, ETA and car, and picks one within 60 s.
- [ ] Offers recorded in `ride_offers`; accept is atomic.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
