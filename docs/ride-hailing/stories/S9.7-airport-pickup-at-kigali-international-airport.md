## User story
**As a** visitor landing in Kigali,
**I want** to pre-book a ride or driver for my arrival with my flight number,
**so that** someone is waiting for me when I land.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Airport is a preset pickup with a defined meeting point and instructions.
- [ ] I can schedule a pickup with flight number; the driver is notified of the time.
- [ ] Driver can display my name on a sign (*Meet & greet* option with configurable fee).
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
