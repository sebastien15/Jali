## User story
**As a** rider,
**I want** to pay my driver in cash or MoMo directly,
**so that** I can ride from day one without card setup.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Rider picks preferred method before requesting; driver sees it on the request card.
- [ ] MoMo option shows the driver's MoMo number/name after completion.
- [ ] Driver confirms received method at completion; disputes can be reported from history.
- [ ] Strings translated (EN/FR/RW/SW) and colours from `C.xxx` where UI is touched
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, with ownership checks in the controller
