## User story
**As a** company,
**I want** a company account where employees ride on company billing with policies,
**so that** staff transport is easy to manage and invoice.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Company admin invites employees; policies (hours, max fare, vehicle classes).
- [ ] Monthly invoice; employee rides use business profile (S15.2).
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
