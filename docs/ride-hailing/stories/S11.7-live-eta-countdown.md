## User story
**As a** rider,
**I want** an ETA that updates continuously while the driver approaches and during the trip,
**so that** I know exactly when to go outside and when I'll arrive.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] ETA recalculated at least every 30 s from the driver's position and route.
- [ ] Shows arrival clock time during the trip (*Arrive 14:32*).
- [ ] Push when the driver is 1 minute away.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
