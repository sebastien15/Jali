## User story
**As a** professional,
**I want** to switch between personal and business profiles,
**so that** work rides are separated and reported automatically.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Business profile with its own payment method and receipt email.
- [ ] Monthly CSV/PDF report of business rides.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
