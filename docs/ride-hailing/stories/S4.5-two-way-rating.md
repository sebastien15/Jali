## User story
**As a** rider or driver,
**I want** to rate the other person 1–5 stars with optional tags and comment,
**so that** good drivers and riders are rewarded and bad behaviour is visible.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Rating prompt appears after completion; can be skipped and done later from history.
- [ ] Tags: e.g. *Clean car, Safe driving, Friendly, Knows the way* / *On time, Polite*.
- [ ] One rating per person per ride (unique constraint).
- [ ] Driver's `rating_avg` and `rating_count` update; shown on nearby cards.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Routes inside `auth:sanctum` + `permission:<x>`, ownership checked in the controller, feature tests added
