## User story
**As a** first-time rider,
**I want** a 3-screen intro explaining driver-set prices and how to ride,
**so that** I understand what makes Jali different.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Shown once; skippable; available again from Help.
- [ ] Explains: choose your driver's price, PIN to start, safety features.
- [ ] Passes the UX quality checklist (`docs/ride-hailing/UX_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`)
